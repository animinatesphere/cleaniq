CleanIQ Worker App - Architecture & Flow Documentation

This document outlines the architecture, user flows, and technical requirements for the CleanIQ Worker Mobile App (iOS & Android). This app connects your hired cleaners to incoming bookings based on their location.

1. Authentication & Onboarding Flow (How Workers Login)

To maintain security and quality control, workers cannot sign up freely on the app. Instead, we use an Admin-Invitation Model.

  1. Application & Interview: The worker applies via a form on your main website. You conduct the interview.
  2. Admin Approval: Once you decide to hire them, you go to your CleanIQ Admin Dashboard and click "Add Worker".
  3. Account Generation: The backend creates a Worker Profile and generates a secure, random Temporary Password.
  4. Welcome Email/SMS: The system sends the iOS/Android app links and their Login Credentials (Email + Temp Password).
  5. First Login: The worker logs into the mobile app.
  6. Mandatory Setup: The app forces them to change their password, upload a photo, and enable GPS location.

2. Job Broadcasting & Acceptance Flow (Order Management)

When a customer books a cleaning, the system automatically routes that booking to the right workers.

  1. New Booking: A customer books. Status: Pending.
  2. Geo-Targeted Broadcast: The backend finds active workers within a specific radius (e.g., 10 miles).
  3. Push Notification: The app sends an instant Push Notification: "🚨 New Job Available!"
  4. Job Feed: Workers open the app and see the job details (pay, area, rooms). Exact house number is hidden.
  5. Acceptance: The first worker to tap "Accept Job" gets assigned.
  6. System Update: The booking updates to Assigned. It disappears from other workers' apps. The worker now sees the full address and entry instructions.

3. Admin Dashboard Integration

  • Worker Management Tab: View all hired workers, status, ratings, and completed jobs.
  • Booking Modal Update: The booking details show the "Assigned Worker" photo and name.
  • Manual Override: Admin can manually un-assign and re-assign workers if needed.
