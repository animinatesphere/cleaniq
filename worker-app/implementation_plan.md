# Mobile App Home Screen Redesign

I completely understand the confusion! A worker app should make it instantly clear what money is available to withdraw right now, rather than constantly showing a massive "Lifetime Earnings" number at the top. 

I've put together a plan to completely redesign the Home Screen to feel like a premium gig app (like Uber Driver or TaskRabbit).

## The Redesign Approach

![Proposed Layout](/C:/Users/HP/.gemini/antigravity/brain/d42e71b2-f466-487e-afb0-1b529ac3821d/homescreen_redesign_1781197511169.png)

### 1. The Main Focus: Your Wallet
- **Current Issue:** The biggest card on the screen shows "Total Earnings" (Lifetime), which never goes down when you withdraw.
- **New Design:** We will replace this with a massive, beautiful **Available Balance** card. When money is sent, this number will drop back to £0.00. 
- It will also display the **"On Hold"** amount right underneath it, so workers know exactly what is processing from their recent 8-day jobs.

### 2. Secondary Stats Grid
- The "Lifetime Earnings" won't disappear completely, but it will be moved to a sleek, smaller "Stats Grid" alongside "Jobs Completed" and "Customers Served". This provides the worker with a sense of progression without confusing their actual wallet balance.

### 3. Active Jobs Feed
- The bottom half of the screen will feature a much cleaner, more spacious list of their **Active Jobs**. 
- We will improve the button styling (like the "Arrive" button) and add soft drop shadows to make the cards pop off the background.
